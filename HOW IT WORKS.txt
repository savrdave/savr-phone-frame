SAVR VIDEO MACHINE

THE FLOW
1. Claude plans the jams and puts plan.txt in DROP HERE: each jam's hook,
   talking points and what's on screen. Talking points, not a script: say it
   your way.
2. Record. One voice memo: say "jam 7", pause, talk. "jam 8", pause, talk.
   Screen record SAVR and screenshot headlines for the jams that need them.
3. AirDrop everything straight into DROP HERE. No folders, no renaming.
4. Double-click Make Jam. (Stage 1: transcribes, a few minutes.)
5. Tell Claude "jams ready". Claude reads what you actually said, decides the
   cuts, fixes misheard words and picks which visual starts on which word.
6. Double-click Make Jam again. (Stage 2: videos land in READY with a review sheet.)
7. Watch each video once, post, drag it to POSTED.

RECORDING
- Messed up a sentence: say "zebra", pause, say the sentence again from the
  start. Everything from the start of the bad sentence to "zebra" is cut.
- Start a whole jam over: say "jam 7" again. The last one wins.
- Pause before every "jam N" so it's heard as a marker.
- Ums, uhs and long pauses get cut. Normal breaths stay.
- Restarts you don't flag with "zebra", Claude usually catches anyway.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- A jam that fails doesn't stop the others. The review sheet says why.
  Fix it (or tell Claude), double-click Make Jam, and it redoes only those.
- Nothing moves out of DROP HERE until every jam is made.
- No Claude available: run Make Jam with --auto (cuts ums and "zebra"
  restarts, guesses visuals). Expect weaker videos.

PLAN.TXT LINES  (Claude writes these)
07 | screen | Grocery prices are up 33% since 2020
08 | broll | Points aren't saving you money | loyalty card checkout; receipt closeup
09 | headlines2+screen | Every headline says prices went up

FOLDERS YOU TOUCH
DROP HERE    everything going in. DROP HERE/claude is Claude's (transcript, edits).
READY        finished, not posted yet (plus the review sheet)
POSTED       posted. Goes to the Trash by itself after 30 days.

FOLDERS YOU DON'T NEED TO OPEN
ORIGINALS    voice memos, screen recordings, screenshots, and each session's plan
             and edits (ORIGINALS/sessions). Kept forever.
B-ROLL       your clips + stock from Pixabay (B-ROLL/stock).
FINISHED     working files. Go to the Trash by themselves after 30 days.

OTHER BUTTONS
Redo Last Session  puts the last finished session back in DROP HERE, so Make Jam
                   can render it again (after Claude changes the edits).
Frame Videos   a screen recording in an iPhone, end card on the end. For CapCut.
Voice Only     puts your voice preset on audio you already edited in Descript.
Voice Test     hear your voice in every preset.

FIRST TIME ON A NEW MAC
1. Install ffmpeg: brew install ffmpeg   (needs Homebrew, brew.sh)
2. Free Pixabay key (pixabay.com/api/docs, shown once you log in). Put it in
   .engine/keys.json as {"pixabay_key": "your key"}. That file never goes to GitHub.
3. Double-click Make Jam once while online. It sets itself up (a few minutes).
