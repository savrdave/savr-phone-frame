SAVR VIDEO MACHINE

THE ONE FLOW
1. Claude plans the week and puts plan.txt in DROP HERE. It lists every jam,
   what it needs on screen, and the hook.
2. Recording session. Say "jam 7", talk, "jam 8", talk, all in one voice memo.
   For jams marked "screen", screen record SAVR in the same order as the jams.
3. Drop the voice memo in DROP HERE/voice memos and the screen recordings in
   DROP HERE/screen recordings. Don't rename anything.
4. Double-click Make Jam. Finished videos land in READY with captions, visuals,
   hook and the SAVR end card. Named from the hook.
5. Post it, then drag it into POSTED.

FOLDERS YOU TOUCH
DROP HERE    things going in
READY        finished, not posted yet
POSTED       posted. Goes to the Trash by itself after 30 days.

FOLDERS YOU DON'T NEED TO OPEN
ORIGINALS    your raw voice memos and screen recordings. Kept forever.
B-ROLL       your clips + free stock from Pixabay (B-ROLL/stock). Picked automatically.
FINISHED     working files. Go to the Trash by themselves after 30 days.

OTHER BUTTONS
Frame Videos   a screen recording in an iPhone, end card on the end. For editing by hand in CapCut.
Voice Only     puts your voice preset on audio you already edited in Descript.
Voice Test     hear your voice in every preset.

PLAN.TXT LINES
07 | screen | Grocery prices are up 33% since 2020
08 | broll  | Points aren't saving you money
09 | headlines+screen | Every headline says prices went up
broll = automatic. screen = your next screen recording. headlines = files in
DROP HERE/visuals starting with the jam number ("09 a.png").
